\section{Proposed Hybrid ATF-EWF Algorithm}
\label{sec:proposed}

Standard pitch estimation approaches frequently encounter catastrophic failures due to octave halving in time-domain autocorrelation methods or spectral leakage and mainlobe overlap in frequency-domain techniques \cite{decheveigne2002yin, schroeder1968hps}. To resolve these limitations, we propose the Adaptive Time-Frequency Energy-Weighted Fusion (ATF-EWF) framework.

\subsection{Joint Salience Function Formulation}
The proposed architecture integrates temporal correlation properties with spectral harmonic structures through a dynamic weighting function. The fused salience $S_{\text{fused}}(\tau)$ at frame time index $t$ is formulated as:
\begin{equation}
S_{\text{fused}}(\tau)
=
\alpha(t)S_{\text{time}}(\tau)
+
\left(1-\alpha(t)\right)S_{\text{freq}}(\tau)
\end{equation}
where $\alpha(t) \in [0, 1]$ is an adaptive confidence coefficient derived directly from the local frame's spectral flatness (Wiener entropy). In noisy or percussive regions where spectral flatness increases, temporal cues are dynamically down-weighted in favor of harmonic profile matching.

\subsection{Phase and Group Delay Refinement}
To overcome the frequency resolution limits imposed by finite window bin spacing ($\Delta f = f_s / N_{\text{FFT}}$), we incorporate instantaneous phase derivative correction via group delay. The group delay $\tau_g(\omega)$ is computed from the unwrapped phase spectrum:
\begin{equation}
\tau_g(\omega)
=
-\frac{d}{d\omega}
\arg\left\{X(e^{j\omega})\right\}
\end{equation}
This derivative provides sub-bin frequency offsets, significantly reducing cent error during sustained monophonic and polyphonic voicing intervals.

\subsection{Inter-Frame Continuity and Octave Clamping}
To eliminate spurious frame-to-frame octave jumps caused by transient background noise, we apply an inter-frame dynamic programming smoothing step. Transition costs penalize instantaneous frequency ratios falling within the octave error boundaries ($1.8 \le f_t / f_{t-1} \le 2.2$ or $0.45 \le f_t / f_{t-1} \le 0.55$), enforcing trajectory smoothness across consecutive frames.
